## English

Dear Alexandra,

thank you for your enquiry. On Tuesday 27 April 2027 our 25-seat Mercedes-Benz Beluga minibus
with driver is available, and please find attached our quotation for the excursion to La Verna.

• Meeting point: Villa Costanza car park, terminus of tram line T1, right on the A1 motorway
• Departure at 7:45, arrival at La Verna around 9:45
• About seven hours at the sanctuary, with the minibus at your disposal
• Departure at 17:00, back at Villa Costanza around 19:00

As a meeting point we recommend Villa Costanza rather than a spot in the centre: from Santo
Spirito it is about 15 minutes on foot to the Alamanni – Stazione tram stop and then about
20 minutes by tram, so leaving at 7:00 is enough. In return the minibus does not enter the
city: no tourist-coach check point (in Florence it costs € 421 per day), no city traffic and
a start directly on the motorway, with more reliable times both in the morning and on the
evening return.

Total price: € 1,300.00 + VAT 10%, i.e. € 1,430.00 VAT included (about € 57 per person),
including driver, fuel, tolls, parking (Villa Costanza included) and permits. Only the tram tickets and the
driver's lunch are at your charge; the lunch can simply be added to the group's booking.

The quotation also includes some practical advice: the road over the Consuma pass is very
winding, at La Verna (1,100 m) it is cool at the end of April, and the sanctuary's refectory
should be booked early for 25 people. With 25 people the Beluga is full: if the group grows,
please let us know before confirming.

The quotation is valid until 30 October 2026. I remain at your disposal for any clarification.
