Why this costs more than an ordinary local transfer

Montemassi and Ribolla are a few kilometres apart, and from the resort too: the "local" part of the job is short. The real cost is positioning: Ponte Buggianese is about 150 km from Montemassi, so the vehicle covers roughly 300 km just to get there and back, on top of the local legs. Eleven hours run between the 14:00 transfer and the 1:00 AM return: too long for a trip back to base and out again, too short not to count as a full day committed. The driver has to stay overnight in the area (at the client's charge, not counted in the price) before driving back.

Price built as a blend of mileage (~300 km of positioning, scaled up from Le Filigare's € 500/50 km with a declining per-km rate over the longer distance) and hours engaged (eleven hours between outbound and return, well beyond the 5-hour day-at-disposal benchmark). Result: € 2,300.00 net, kept on the high side as usual — Girolamo can bring it down if he judges it right.

The critical point: the return time

Departure from the venue set for 1:00 AM: with only a 20-25 minute transfer to Montemassi, getting back within 02:00 (the € 250.00 surcharge threshold) holds only if nothing runs late. At weddings that is an optimistic assumption. Flagged to the client in the quotation's notes.
